Executive Summary

Solution Blueprint: How the Commercial Lending Suite Composes on IIF v1.5

JazzX Commercial Lending Suite · Engineering Corpus v1.2 · July 2026 · Proprietary & Confidential

WHAT THIS CORPUS IS

The Functional Requirements corpus (FRD-00…07) says what the Suite does. This Solution Blueprint corpus says how the Suite is constructed on the Institutional Intelligence Framework (IIF) v1.5 — how the functional capabilities are realized as cognitive-mode compositions, skills, execution profiles, domain packs, and surface bindings, sitting on the IIF substrate. It is the bridge between the functional backlog and the platform architecture. It is written for engineers and architects who will build the Suite on the framework, and it assumes the FRDs as its functional input.

0a. Strategic rationale — why composition on IIF is the moat

The Suite is not an application with AI features; it is a composition of governed capabilities on a reusable institutional-intelligence substrate. That distinction is the strategy. Every hard problem a regulated institution has with AI — who is accountable, what evidence is required, when autonomy is permitted, how behavior is audited, how improvement is governed — is solved once in the framework and inherited by every capability, every surface, and every domain pack, rather than re-solved per feature.

The narrowing chain — Charter → Domain Pack Specification → Surface Binding Annex → Certified Client Overlay → Execution Profile — is what lets an institution adopt autonomy incrementally and provably. A bank does not buy a black box; it ratifies, cell by cell, what the machine may do, under which evidence floors, with which accountable humans — and it can demonstrate all of it to a regulator from the artifacts themselves.

The composition is also the commercial moat. New domains (agency multifamily ships in this corpus as exactly such an overlay), new institutions (client overlays and execution profiles), and new surfaces reuse the same governed substrate — so the marginal cost of the next capability falls, while accumulated outcome data, calibration telemetry, and the judgment ledger make the installed base progressively harder to displace.

1. The framework in one page

IIF v1.5 is an architecture for turning domain judgment into governed, self-improving, scalable autonomous operations. Its foundations, all of which the Suite inherits rather than re-invents:

The three pillars.

  • 1. Governed — autonomy operates inside explicit boundaries of policy, evidence, identity, permissions, data visibility, safety, privacy, and human accountability; auditable by construction.

  • 2. Self-improving — outcomes, overrides, defects, drift signals, and human corrections improve future behavior, but only through a versioned, tested, traceable, governed process; improvement is earned and gated, never silently declared.

  • 3. Scalable beyond individual expertise — expert judgment becomes an institutional asset; capacity grows without growing senior-expert headcount one-for-one.

The nine non-negotiable design principles (architectural invariants every Pack, surface, overlay, and profile must satisfy — a violation is not certifiable):

  • 1. Evidence-First Outputs · 2. Policy-as-Runtime · 3. Trace Is a Product Artifact · 4. Evaluation Before Promotion · 5. Data Sovereignty by Default · 6. Human Accountability Remains · 7. Composability Through Canonical Contracts · 8. Outcome-Linked Learning Loops · 9. Versioned Behavior; No Silent Changes.

The five canonical objects (the closed chain every consequential action produces, consumes, or links to): Policy (what is allowed/required/prohibited, as a constraint that runs) → Evidence (what is true/supported/fresh/sufficient, with a confidence verdict) → Decision (what was judged/recommended/selected and why, with the named accountable human) → Trace (what happened: steps, tools, approvals, handoffs, overrides, side effects, versions) → Outcome (what resulted, linked back to the decision and trace). The chain is closed.

The thirteen cognitive modes in five families (the institutional division of labor):

  • THINK (the brain): Reasoner, Investigator, Simulator

  • TRUST (the immune system): Governor, Verifier, Sentinel

  • EXECUTE (the hands): Conductor, Optimizer

  • INTERACT (the voice): Narrator, Influencer, Negotiator

  • EVOLVE (the memory): Curator, Evaluator

They compose into a loop — THINK → TRUST → EXECUTE → INTERACT → EVOLVE — whose outcomes feed back through EVOLVE to sharpen the next cycle.

The four surface types (the minimum set of distinct governance shapes): Assistant (persona-centered foreground for one human role), Workspace (case-centered, persistent, collaborative, multi-person), API/Service (programmatic surface consumed by other systems — the Expert-style interface), Automation (background execution triggered by event/schedule/threshold; defining concern is side effect and irreversibility).

Governed autonomy is a ladder earned at the decision cell, not the product average: L0 Assist · L1 Recommend · L2 Execute-with-approval · L3 Policy-bounded autonomy · L4 Self-improvement-within-bounds — earned through four gate families (Conformance, Launch, Promotion, Recertification), narrow, observable, reversible, revocable on signal.

2. The narrowing chain — where the Suite plugs in

Institutional Intelligence Charter (strategy, pillars, principles, canonical chain, modes, surfaces)

│

Architecture Decision Memo (D1–D63: the settled decisions every doc cites)

│

Substrate specifications (Skill Architecture · Cognitive-Mode contracts · Canonical Object Schema · Minimum Interoperability Spec · Shared Entity Ontology · Governed Knowledge Assets · IPDV · Observability · Versioning)

│

DOMAIN PACK ── the unit of the moat: the commercial-lending domain intelligence

cl_of (Origination & Fulfillment: VP-1…VP-4) cl_sp (Servicing & Portfolio: VP-5…VP-6)

│

SURFACE BINDING ANNEX (SBA) ── binds a Pack's capabilities to one operational surface

(per surface or role-pattern: object rights, action classes, mode composition, autonomy ceiling)

│

CERTIFIED CLIENT OVERLAY ── narrows the Pack + SBA envelope to one institution (e.g., a specific bank)

(jurisdictions, role mappings, thresholds, approved evidence sources & connectors, configured posture)

│

│

EXECUTION PROFILE (EP) ── the final narrowing: what is actually live in one deployment

(activated modes, capability catalog, skill bundle, autonomy postures, version bundle, non-widening attestation)

│

Runbooks / Ops — the EP records live posture beneath the SBA + Overlay intersection; each layer only narrows

IIF v1.5 is organized as a narrowing chain: each layer only narrows the one above it (adds constraints, never widens authority). The Suite is a set of artifacts placed on this chain.

The Suite is therefore not a monolithic application; it is: two domain packs (cl_of, cl_sp) built on the substrate, exposed through surface binding annexes for each surface or role-pattern, deployed via execution profiles, optionally narrowed by a client overlay. Each FRD's functional content becomes capabilities in a pack; each capability binds to modes; each surface binds a subset of capabilities with an autonomy ceiling.

4. Why this composition (design rationale)

  • Modes make judgment governable. By expressing each capability as a composition of a small, fixed set of modes — each with a defined input/output contract, evidence standard, governance posture, and trace behavior — the Suite avoids becoming an "undifferentiated agent blob." Authority is constrained at the right boundary (e.g., Governor enforces the human-only gate on CI-DC-11 rating), and each mode is tuned once and reused across every workflow that needs it (e.g., the Verifier tie-out logic serves VP-2 spreading and VP-6 covenant testing).

  • Packs are the moat. The commercial-lending domain intelligence — the metric definitions, the decision classes, the covenant library, the evidence admissibility rules, the archetype/property analytics — lives in the domain pack, not in generic model prompts. That is what makes the Suite defensible and what lets it compound: overrides and outcomes improve the pack's assets through the EVOLVE loop.

  • The canonical chain makes it examinable. Because every decision produces Policy/Evidence/Decision/Trace/Outcome, the audit trail the FRDs rely on (XF-6, exam readiness in FRD-06) is a structural byproduct, not a separate build.

  • The narrowing chain makes it safe to deploy per client. A specific bank's policy, authority model, and risk appetite are applied through a Certified Client Overlay and Execution Profile that only narrow the pack — the same pack serves many banks without forks (data sovereignty by abstraction, principle 5).
