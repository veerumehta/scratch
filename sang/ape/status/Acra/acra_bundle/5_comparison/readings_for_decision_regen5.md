# Readings to decide: where the two job sets resolve an open guideline question differently

Source: `jobs_vs_kstudio_regen5.xlsx`, sheet "4 Thresholds and readings". Each item below is a point the guideline itself marks "Acra confirmation requested" (Q-nn). The choice is an underwriting decision, not a model difference. Three options per item: **refer** (the job states the question and returns a conditional outcome), **fill** (the job applies a stated reading as the answer), **keep both** (the job tests each reading and is conditional only when they differ).

Default proposed for every item: **keep both, or refer; never fill silently.** The exceptions are noted where one reading is only stricter than the other.
