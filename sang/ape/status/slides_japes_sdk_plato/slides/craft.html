Design the pack before running a transaction

  1. Find the policies and author them as canonical Policy objects
  2. Find the playbooks: how experts actually work a case
  3. Design the ontology: the domain's entities and relations
  4. Design the workflow: the dominant loop and its side checks
  5. Specialize the modes by tuning, not by writing new code

THE SDK SUPPLIES

A policy expert configured with the pack's policies, no subclass needed

Thirteen cognition modes, tuned per pack with markdown

A Conductor that runs the declared workflow, so no pack hand-writes orchestration

Japes SDK and Plato

10

Crafting is design-first. The team's work is domain knowledge: what the rules are, how an expert works a case, what the entities are. The SDK supplies the machinery: the policy expert is configured rather than subclassed, the modes (investigator, verifier, reasoner, governor, narrator and others) are tuned with markdown, and the Conductor runs the workflow.
