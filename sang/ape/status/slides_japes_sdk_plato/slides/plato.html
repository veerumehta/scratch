Japes SDK and Plato

06

Every route sits under /api/v1, like the sibling services. Plato keeps its own Postgres control plane for assistant configuration, the pack store, runs, feedback and evaluation. The boot contract is one table that decides what each misconfiguration does: a serving replica comes up and reports, a job exits on a documented code.
