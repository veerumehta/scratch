This is the key idea for the build path. A team does not write a service; it authors a pack. An assistant pack is a profile: persona, skills, what knowledge it is grounded in. A domain pack is a workflow: the policies, the vocabulary, the metrics, and which conductor runs the case. A domain pack can also carry an assistant over the cases it produces.
