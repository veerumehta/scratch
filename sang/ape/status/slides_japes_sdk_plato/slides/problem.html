Japes SDK and Plato

02

The services built before japes are v1-shaped: each one solved LLM calls, tracing, knowledge access and governance its own way. Japes takes the patterns those services proved and makes them the one consistent default. Plato goes a step further: for many apps there is no new service at all, only a pack published to Plato.
